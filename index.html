About

BEV Family Office was set up in 2023 to manage Benjamin Radomski's own investments.

Benjamin is from Stockholm. He studied in London and spent ten years in finance, marketing and communications, working with clients such as KPMG and HSBC. He has since founded and built more than a dozen companies.

Most new capital now goes into funds. Earlier on, we invested directly in early-stage companies.
